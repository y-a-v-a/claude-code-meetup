# Ruud van Elk

- Role: Head of Sport Science & Analytics, PSV
- Talk: A platform built with Claude that brings together movement analysis, match data and training design
- How it works in daily practice at one of Europe's top football clubs, with a live demo.

Our next speaker has spent almost fifteen years at PSV, starting as an intern in 2012 and now leading Sport Science & Analytics. He has explained football data at Harvard, FC Barcelona, and to the KNVB's top coaches. He says about a third of a football season is luck, and his job is the rest. Earlier this year he set out to build a small form to fix missing data, and a few days later he had a full web application, built with Claude Code. Please welcome Ruud van Elk.
