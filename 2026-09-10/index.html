Why are you laughing?

  • "What are you watching on YouTube?"
  • Not YouTube. Claude Code.
In March 2025, just a week or two after Claude code was released as research preview, our family was sitting at weekend breakfast and i was looking at my laptop on and off, and at some point i started laughing. My wife asked me: what are you laughing about? What are you watching on YouTube? And though it indeed looked like i was watching a video, i was watching the output of Claude code. And i had to laugh. It was a rich laugh, bittersweet with a little anxiousness, a laugh i seem to laugh when looking at good comedy too. Hence my wife's remark, i suppose.

So… what is a skill?

skills deep dive

A skill is…

  • An extension of the AI agent
  • A distilled set of capabilities
  • A group of related operations

Discovered little by little

1 · idle Only the description is loaded
→
2 · trigger Your message matches, or you type /skill
→
3 · act Agent reads the rest, then acts
Skills are discovered progressively, so "little by little". Initially only a description of what the skill can do or what it is used for. When the agent is triggered by a user message on the skill's description, or when called explicitly with a slash-command, then the agent will start to discover the skill further and take actions where needed.

Why bother?

  • No more retyping instructions
  • Isolated, focused topics
  • Can include scripts, even Office files
Benefits: reduce retyping of instructions, reduction of repetitive work. Operate on isolated topics. Can be enriched with scripting/coding capabilities. Can operate on office software file types too.
