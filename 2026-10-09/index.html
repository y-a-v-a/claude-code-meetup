The Hidden AI Vanguards

Stay on the lookout for that accelerating coworker, and foster them!

Vincent Bruijn – 2026-10-09

An AI optimist

2 · after the initial rollout

AI a replacement for Google...?

  • How can we progress AI use beyond search++
  • Some people just don't think about what they could ask Claude
  • But luckily some people are always more tool-curious

Freedom to experiment

  • A few power users accelerate: they take the freedom to experiment
  • They understand the time invested will pay off

  • Others don't feel that freedom, or don't get it
  • They work to expectations, task by task
  • Their role exists in projects, routines and structure

Convince managers
to give people
time to experiment

3 · spot them

Who might be the hidden AI vanguard?

A combination of these signs. The vanguard:

  • Runs out of tokens on a standard seat
  • Asks for Connectors: "how can I reach this data?"
  • Asks how to enable a self built tool for other colleagues
  • Suddenly brings awesome slide decks to meetings
  • Moves beyond their role

Heavy use is not always good use

Be careful with complaints about limits. Ask how they hit them.

case · translation A sheet of 10,000 rows by 8 columns, into 6 languages, in one web chat. Right idea, wrong tool. We asked them to ask Claude for a token-efficient way. Now: Cowork with a set of prompts.
case · store planning An operational planning tool for multiple stores, built in Excel. It almost became a full SaaS app inside a spreadsheet. We slimmed down the business case and moved it to Cowork.

A vanguard moves beyond their role (1)

  • She starts coding
    • "Never done before, but I think I'm getting there"
    • "I wanted better oversight, so I built this"
  • They Deliver mockups in the company style guide
    • "I couldn't find the style guide,
      so pointed Claude to our site"

A vanguard moves beyond their role (2)

  • He delivers reports with new insights on existing data
    • "I found old reports in my archive, and tought..."
    • "I had a long wish for analyzing XYZ"
  • They spot inefficient workflows and finds solutions on their own
    • "I was fed up with doing X, so I built skill Y to solve it"
    • "We saved $$$ by comparing last year's contract with current proposal"

Find the vanguards!

  • The ones who embrace AI in novel ways
  • The ones where AI's capabilities landed
  • The ones that surprise themselves
  • Let's use their knowledge
  • Let's learn from their attitudes

4 · foster them

What do we do with the vanguards?

  • Feed their curiosity with new tools
  • Discuss their current workflows
  • Discuss their wishes: "I wish I could X, then Y, and even Z"
  • Share how we think about applying AI

They usually have a vision

Licenses

  • Start with standard seats, only then consider premium
  • Be willing to upgrade a vanguard's seat
  • So they not only speed up, but also explore more

Room to explore

  • Allow different vendors: ChatGPT alongside Claude
    • Their models and desktop apps are of similar quality
  • Challenge them:
    • "What does your Claude do during lunch?"
    • "Fix these ticket with as much AI as possible"
    • "Find a way to security check your app"

5 · spread adoption

Put the vanguards to work

  • Give them an extra role: visit other departments
  • Let them be the drivers and the challengers
  • Put their attitude to work
    • Creative people who dare to experiment, and tell it well
    • The techie who explains in plain words why token efficiency matters

A head of tech with 100 staff hired 3 to 4 people just to walk around, nudge and explain what AI could automate

Small groups over plenary

  • People feel overwhelmed in big group sessions
  • Showcase to managers, so they spread it within their team
  • Let managers point out where their team can improve
  • Focus on one person's tasks

Serve the food on the plate, not in the mouth

Don't ask me, ask Claude

  • A question comes up in a meeting?
    • Check it with Claude, right now
    • Not "let's review after the meeting"
  • Show a live demo on your own company data
  • No fear of "technical": challenge your colleague to instruct Claude like a coworker
  • Share "Routines"
    • Legal: a weekly mail on accessibility court cases
    • Tech: a weekly CVE check
    • Trade: weekly online competitor compare

FrAIday

What task did I do this week that I could have done with AI?
  • Share knowledge every Friday in a shared chat group
  • Let vanguards help with people who dislike manual tasks
  • Learn so people start to imagine what AI can automate
    • show, don't tell

6 · guardrails

The vanguards know the limits

  • Their job is to show where the limits and risks are
  • They have a sense of what AI can and can't do
  • And a sense of the risks of applying AI to certain systems and data

"Can I get access to Google BigQuery?"

  • At first sight: the solution. It is not.
  • Better get to understand the real business case
  • As the information is often already available elsewhere
  • And querying it freely can become very expensive
  • Beside, access to everything is not always the answer

Enable playing fields

  • Safe spaces to experiment
    • Enable Claude features progressively
  • Make people aware of the consequences of
    • heavy queries
    • token consuming prompts
    • model selection (Haiku vs. Sonnet vs. Opus)

7 · takeaway

A few weeks after a rollout

  1. Identify the vanguards
  2. Foster (and challenge!) them generously
  3. Use their experience for the next step
